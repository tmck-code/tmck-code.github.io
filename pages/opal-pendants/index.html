Opal Pendants — Australian Shortlist

Australian opal pendants and necklaces with a dark body (black, semi-black, dark crystal, dark boulder, or a doublet or triplet on a dark backing) and three or more play-of-colour hues. Candidates have no diamonds or other stones named by the seller. Pieces that are sold out or have stones are listed separately as worth an enquiry. Prices in AUD; stock as last checked.

Shortlist Research notes

Specifications are seller marketing copy and have not been independently verified. “No stones named” means the seller text names none — not an explicit “no diamonds” statement. Prices in AUD. Stock (one-off pieces) and prices as checked 14 October 2026 and may change.
